## 2. Detailed Tier Specifications

### STARTER Tier (Free)
* **Target Audience:** Micro-vendors, independent home-chefs, beginner tiffin aunties with 10–18 neighborhood customers.
* **Strategic Objective:** Frictionless top-of-funnel vendor acquisition. Proves RUXS value with zero financial commitment.
* **Upgrade Trigger:** When adding the 21st subscriber, vendor dashboard displays a soft prompt to upgrade to Growth tier.

### GROWTH Tier (₹499 / month)
* **Target Audience:** Established single-kitchen operators, mid-sized water suppliers, neighborhood doodhwalas.
* **Value Delivered:**
  * Eliminates 15–20 hours of manual monthly billing reconciliation.
  * Recovers ₹3,000–₹6,000 in prevented food waste and recovered water cans every month.
  * ROI for vendor is typically **6x to 12x** the ₹499 monthly fee.

### PRO / HUB Tier (₹1,499 / month)
* **Target Audience:** Commercial cloud kitchens, multi-tempo water distributors, large laundry hubs delivering to 200–1,000+ homes across entire suburbs.
* **Value Delivered:**
  * Multi-driver route assignment across distinct geographical zones.
  * Automated UPI AutoPay e-mandates on the 1st of every month.
  * Direct CSV/Excel export for chartered accountant tax filing.
