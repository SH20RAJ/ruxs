# Target Users & Roles: RUXS

> **Canonical Document:** This document provides the high-level role summary. For in-depth persona narratives, see [target-users.md](file:///Users/shaswatraj/Desktop/ruxx/docs/product/target-users.md).

## Core Role Models

1. **CUSTOMER (Household Account Owner):** Subscribes, manages delivery preferences, confirms/skips daily orders via WhatsApp/Web, inspects the Digital Khata, and pays monthly invoices.
2. **HOUSEHOLD MEMBER:** Roommate, family member, or co-living tenant who shares domestic services, participates in expense splitting, and views daily fulfillment status.
3. **VENDOR ADMIN:** Local business owner (kitchen, dairy, water depot) who sets cutoffs, configures recurring catalogs, manages customers, tracks returnable assets, and dispatches run sheets.
4. **DELIVERY STAFF:** Field riders and boys who execute doorstep deliveries, record asset exchanges (empty vs full), and upload proof of delivery.
5. **PLATFORM ADMIN:** RUXS internal operations staff who oversee vendor onboarding, dispute escalations, and platform-wide metrics.
