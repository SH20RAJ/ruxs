# Feature Specification: Vendor Operations & Roster Management

**Classification:** `[CONFIRMED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT]`

## 1. Goal
Provide local service vendors with lightweight, mobile-first management tools for customer rosters, service catalogs, cutoff configurations, and delivery staff assignments.

## 2. Capabilities
* **Customer Roster:** Searchable list of subscribers with live delivery status, active subscriptions, and running Khata balances.
* **Service Configurations:** Multi-shift support, cutoff timing rules, late cancellation policies, and product SKU pricing.
* **Delivery Staff Assignment:** Assigning drivers to specific apartment societies and dispatching digital run sheets.
* **Manual Order Actions:** Vendor override capability to manually record a skip or extra order requested over a phone call.
