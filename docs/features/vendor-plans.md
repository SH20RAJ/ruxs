## Summary of SaaS Quotas
* **STARTER (Free):** Up to 20 active subscribers, 1 driver login, standard WhatsApp polling.
* **GROWTH (₹499/mo):** Up to 150 active subscribers, 3 driver logins, asset tracking, multi-shift cutoffs.
* **PRO / HUB (₹1,499/mo):** Unlimited subscribers, unlimited drivers, route optimization, priority UPI reconciliation.
